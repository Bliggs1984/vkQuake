Before you install Quake RT, check you have:

  - Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)

  - An NVIDIA RTX graphics card (RTX 2060 or newer).
    Recent AMD cards (RX 6000 series or newer) should also work.
    Older GTX cards will not work.

  - Quake, installed from Steam (or GOG / Epic Games Store).
    Quake RT uses your own copy of the game.

  - Up-to-date graphics drivers (NVIDIA App > Drivers > Download).

After installing, double-click the "Quake RT" icon on your desktop.
Start Steam first if you want your Quake achievements to unlock.

A "How to play" guide opens when the installer finishes and is also
in the Start menu under Quake RT.
