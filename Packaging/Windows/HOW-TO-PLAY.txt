QUAKE RT - HOW TO INSTALL AND PLAY
==================================

Quake RT is the original Quake with modern ray-traced lighting (real-time
light, shadows and reflections) and NVIDIA DLSS. It uses your own copy of
Quake, so you need to own the game.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------

  * A Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC.
  * An NVIDIA RTX graphics card (RTX 2060 or newer). Recent AMD cards
    (RX 6000 series or newer) should also work. Older GTX cards will NOT work.
  * Quake installed from Steam (it is the normal "Quake" you buy on Steam
    today). Quake from GOG or the Epic Games Store also works.
  * Up-to-date graphics drivers. For NVIDIA, open the "NVIDIA App" and click
    "Download" on the Drivers page if there is an update.


INSTALLING
----------

  1. Double-click  QuakeRT-Setup-2.0.0.exe

  2. Windows may show a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC".
     This happens with any program that is not from a big company.
     Click  "More info", then click  "Run anyway".

  3. Click  Next, then Install, then Finish.
     If it asks to install "Microsoft Visual C++", click Yes. That is a
     standard Microsoft component that many games need.

That's it. You now have a "Quake RT" icon on your desktop and in the Start menu.


PLAYING
-------

  1. Start Steam first (so your Quake achievements unlock).
  2. Double-click the "Quake RT" icon.
  3. The first time, if you have a 4K screen, it asks if you want to play
     at 1440p. Click "Yes" - it runs much faster and still looks great.
  4. Pick "Campaign" in the menu to start a new game.

The first start can take up to a minute while it gets things ready.


CONTROLS
--------

  Move ................ W A S D
  Look / aim .......... Mouse
  Shoot ............... Left mouse button
  Jump ................ Space
  Run ................. Shift
  Swim down ........... Ctrl
  Change weapon ....... 1 to 8, or the mouse wheel
  Menu / pause ........ Esc
  Quick save .......... F6
  Quick load .......... F9
  Ray tracing on/off .. F   (press it to see the difference!)

You can change any key in  Options > Key Bindings.


IF IT RUNS SLOWLY
-----------------

Press Esc and go to  Options > Video. Then try these, one at a time:

  * If you have an RTX 2000 or RTX 3000 series card: change "DLSS Model"
    to  DLSS 4 (K).  (The newer 4.5 model is made for RTX 4000/5000 cards.)
  * Change "Nvidia DLSS" from Quality to  Balanced  or  Performance.

Press Esc to go back to the game.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

  * It says it can't find Quake:
      Install Quake from Steam, start it once from Steam, then try again.

  * It crashes, shows an error about Vulkan or ray tracing, or the screen
    stays black:
      Update your graphics driver (see "What you need" above) and restart
      the PC. If you have an older GTX card, it won't work, sorry.

  * Your saved games and settings are kept in:
      %APPDATA%\QuakeRT
      (paste that into the address bar of a File Explorer window)


EXTRAS
------

  * The expansion packs that come with Steam's Quake (Scourge of Armagon,
    Dissolution of Eternity, Dimension of the Past, Dimension of the Machine,
    Dawn of the Machine) are in the  Mods  menu.

  * Want to start Quake RT from Steam's Play button (with the Steam overlay
    and "Playing Quake" showing for your friends)?
      1. Start menu > Quake RT > "Steam launch option". A small text file
         opens with one line in it. Copy that whole line.
      2. In Steam, right-click Quake > Properties. Paste the line into the
         "Launch Options" box and close the window.
    Now pressing Play on Quake in Steam starts Quake RT. To undo it, empty
    that box again.


UNINSTALLING
------------

Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Quake RT > Uninstall.
Your saved games stay in %APPDATA%\QuakeRT in case you come back.
